Composting Tips 🌿

Learn how to compost better.

🍌

Fruit & Veggie Scraps

Always compostable. Cut them small for faster breakdown. Avoid oily or salted scraps.

📰

Paper & Cardboard

Shredded paper and torn cardboard are excellent brown materials. Avoid glossy paper.

☕

Coffee Grounds

Coffee grounds and filters are great green material. They enrich compost with nitrogen.

🚫

Avoid These

Meat, dairy, oily food, and pet waste are non-compostable and attract pests.

💧

Moisture Balance

Keep compost as moist as a wrung-out sponge. Too dry = slow; too wet = smelly.

🔄

Turn Regularly

Turn your pile every 1–2 weeks to aerate. Oxygen speeds up decomposition significantly.
